About

Where I'm coming from

I work across CommonAI CIC, the Scaling Inference Lab and High Assurance AI, where I lead the build of a novel foundation model trained from scratch on our own in-house cluster. Day to day that means as much systems work as research: comparing topologies across five HPC clusters, standing up the software stack on a new one, and then using it to run the experiments. Alongside that I work on AI safety and control — writing protocols and looking into white-boxing methods for catching suspicious agent behaviour.

I started out in Vilnius at IBM, first designing dialogue flows for virtual assistants and then moving into data science, where I integrated language models into a translation product, trained a vision model to spot misaligned machine parts, and kept the backend services behind them healthy. In parallel I was doing research on ECG signals at the Institute of Data Science and Digital Technologies, which turned into two papers on classifying arrhythmia with transformer models.

What keeps pulling me back is the point where the engineering and the science stop being separable — where the answer to a research question depends on how the cluster is wired. That, and the fact that I'd like the work to end up in Lithuania.
